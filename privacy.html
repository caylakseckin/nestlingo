Privacy Policy

How NestLingo handles app preferences, reminders, purchases, support requests and your privacy choices.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Overview

NestLingo is designed for parents and caregivers who want to practice simple daily language activities with young children. This policy explains what the app stores, how it's used, and the choices you have. NestLingo is built around offline, parent-guided activities rather than child-directed social features.

What the app stores

NestLingo keeps your preferences on your device: the interface language, the learning language, activity progress, your word-journal notes, reminder settings, profile name and photo, and premium status. This is what makes the app work and adapt to your family. Your profile photo stays on your device and is not uploaded.

Audio

Word and phrase recordings are delivered from our audio host so playback is high quality. Requesting an audio file involves a standard network request; we don't use it to build a profile of you.

Children's privacy

NestLingo is intended to be used by a parent or caregiver. Children shouldn't enter personal information into the app, and the app has no child-directed social features, chat or user-generated public content.

How information is used

  • To provide the language activities and remember progress.
  • To deliver optional daily reminders if you enable notifications.
  • To process premium access through Apple's in-app purchases.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.

Notifications

If enabled, NestLingo may send a daily reminder about the next suggested activity. You can turn reminders off any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store full payment card details. Purchase status is used only to unlock premium features such as the full activity library and additional learning languages.

Data retention

Device preferences and journal notes stay on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove locally stored data from your device.
  • Email us to ask for help with data access or deletion.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always posted on this page with the updated date.

Contact

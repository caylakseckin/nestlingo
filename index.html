How it works

Three steps, and the phone goes down

NestLingo isn't another app your child stares at. It hands you a small, ready moment and gets out of the way.

1

Hear the word

Every word and phrase plays as real recorded speech in your chosen language — clear, human, never robotic.

2

Put the phone down

Each activity is built to be done away from the screen, in about five minutes, during something you're already doing.

3

Say it together

Try the word in the real moment — pouring water, pulling on a sock, waving goodbye. That's where it sticks.

Questions

What parents ask before starting

What is NestLingo?
A screen-free iOS app that helps parents teach a baby or toddler a second language through everyday moments. It gives you a word and short phrases in real recorded speech; you say them out loud during dressing, meals, play and bedtime, so your child hears the language from you rather than from a screen.
What ages is it for?
Babies and toddlers, roughly ages one to four. The parent uses the app and does the activity with the child, and the guidance adapts to the child's age and language stage.
Which languages can my child learn?
Six: English, German and Turkish are free, and French, Spanish and Italian come with Premium. Every word and phrase is available as real recorded audio in each language.
Do I need to speak the language myself?
No. Every word and phrase plays as clear recorded human speech, so you can teach a language you're still learning without worrying about pronunciation.
Is it really screen-free for my child?
Yes. The app is made for you to use. You listen to the word, put the phone down, and try it together in the real moment — so your child learns without screen time.
How much does it cost?
NestLingo is free to download and includes a free 7-day starter plan plus free activities that stay free. Premium unlocks the full library of 111 activities and the French, Spanish and Italian learning languages.

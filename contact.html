Contact us

For questions about Baby Talk, support requests, privacy questions, bug reports, or purchase help, contact us by email.

Email support

What to include

  • Your app version.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • A short description of the issue.
  • Screenshots if they help explain the problem.

Privacy requests

For privacy or data deletion requests, use the same email address and include “Privacy Request” in your message. If the app supports account creation, account deletion should also be available from inside the app.

Important

Please do not send sensitive personal information, payment card details, or private child information by email unless it is strictly necessary for support.
